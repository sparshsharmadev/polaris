To avoid a website looking "vibe coded," you must enforce strict design constraints and prioritize specificity over generic AI defaults.  The primary goal is to replace the statistical average of AI training data with intentional, brand-specific decisions.

Visual and Structural Constraints

Eliminate Default Aesthetics: Strictly avoid purple gradients, glowing blobs, glassmorphism, and excessive rounded corners.  These are the most common visual tells of unreviewed AI output.
Restrict Color Usage: Pick one dominant color, one accent, and one neutral.  Avoid using colored borders, side tabs, or gradients on every section, as this creates visual chaos and cancels out emphasis.
Simplify Layouts: Remove nested cards and unnecessary wrappers. Use whitespace and typography for grouping instead of default feature grids.  Avoid the standard "hero, features, social proof, pricing" sequence if it doesn't serve the specific product.
Use Real Evidence: Replace fake testimonials, generic AI faces, and abstract icon grids with real product screenshots, specific data points, and concrete examples. 
Content and Copywriting Rules

Be Specific, Not Polished: Replace vague SaaS buzzwords like "Unlock powerful insights" or "Build your dreams" with product-specific verbs that describe exactly what the tool does.  If a sentence could apply to 500 other products, rewrite it.
Remove Decorative Elements: Delete emojis from headings, unnecessary sparkles, and generic pill badges (e.g., "AI Powered," "Fast") that do not add functional information. 
Define a Design System: Before prompting AI, write down decisions for theme, color values, typography (avoiding default Inter font if possible), and tone. Provide these specific instructions to the AI rather than asking for "modern and clean." 
Technical and UX Hygiene

Ensure Accessibility: Check for contrast ratios (minimum 4.5:1 for normal text), proper heading hierarchies, and keyboard navigation support, which are often neglected in vibe-coded sites. 
Add Intentional Motion: Use animations only to explain state or guide attention (e.g., showing a scan progress). Avoid infinite glows, pulsing dots, or moving gradients that serve no functional purpose. 
Validate Input and Security: Ensure all forms have proper client and server-side validation to prevent XSS or SQL injection, and verify that no secrets or API keys are exposed in the client bundle.
